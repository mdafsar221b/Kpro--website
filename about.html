Engagement Model

Why We Champion the "True Fixed Cost Model"

Hourly billing aligns a consultancy's financial motivations against the client's goal. The longer the developers take, the more the consultancy profits.

Under Kpro's outcome-based model, we invest significant architecture hours upfront to clarify scopes, define schemas, specify delivery milestones, and sign off on a guaranteed cost.

Discuss Your Scoping Needs

1. Risk Absorption

If unexpected engineering challenges appear during sprint development, Kpro absorbs all extra labor expenses, not the client.

2. Strict Delivery Commitments

Scopes are tethered to chronological calendar milestone reviews. We execute with extreme discipline to maintain delivery timelines.

3. Measurable Value

Because project metrics are detailed precisely before writing code, stakeholders can calculate exact ROI projections beforehand.
